Grand Experience

The Ultimate Morocco Expedition

14 Days / 13 Nights

Overview

The complete Morocco experience in one expedition, imperial cities, the blue mountains of the north, and the deep Sahara, all in a single fully private itinerary.

Casablanca → Rabat → Tangier → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga Desert → Todra Gorges → Ouarzazate → Marrakech

Day by Day

Day 1
Arrival in Casablanca
Airport pickup, visit Hassan II Mosque.
Day 2
Casablanca to Rabat
Explore the capital's Kasbah and Mausoleum.
Day 3
Rabat to Tangier
Coastal drive to the gateway of Africa.
Day 4
Tangier to Chefchaouen
Discover the Blue Pearl of the Rif Mountains.
Day 5
Chefchaouen to Fes
Scenic drive south into the imperial city.
Day 6
Fes Guided Tour
Full day in the Medina and traditional workshops.
Day 7
Fes to Merzouga
Via Midelt and the Ziz Valley to the desert edge.
Day 8
Camel Trek & Desert Camp
Sunset camel ride, dinner and night in Erg Chebbi.
Day 9
Merzouga to Todra Gorges
Sunrise in the dunes, then the dramatic Todra canyon.
Day 10
Todra to Ouarzazate
The Dades Valley and the 'Hollywood of Morocco'.
Day 11
Ouarzazate to Marrakech
Cross the High Atlas via Aït Ben Haddou.
Day 12
Marrakech Guided Tour
Full day in the souks, palaces, and Majorelle Garden.
Day 13
Free Day in Marrakech
Optional excursion to the coastal town of Essaouira.
Day 14
Departure
Transfer to the airport for your flight home.

Trip Highlights

  • The complete all-in-one Moroccan experience
  • Imperial capitals and northern coastal gems
  • Blue mountain towns and deep desert dunes
