Overview

Marrakech, the Red City, is Morocco's most famous imperial city — a maze of a medina packed with palaces, mosques, gardens, and the ever-buzzing Jemaa el-Fnaa square. It's a full and rewarding day trip from Agadir or Taghazout, crossing the Atlas Mountains along the way.

Your driver-guide picks you up from your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout in a private, air-conditioned vehicle, crossing the scenic Atlas Mountain road with stops for photos along the way. Once in Marrakech, your guide leads you through Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, the Koutoubia Mosque, Bahia Palace, the Menara Gardens, and the medina souks before the drive back in the evening.

Pickup is at 06:30 AM from Agadir hotels and 06:50 AM from Taghazout, with drop-off back at your hotel between 9:00 and 9:30 PM.
