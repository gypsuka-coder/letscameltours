Privacy Policy

How Let's Camel Tours collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you book a tour, excursion, or activity with us.

Last updated: August 2026

01

Data Collection

When you make a booking or enquiry through our website, email, or WhatsApp, we collect information you provide directly, such as your full name, email address, phone number, nationality, travel dates, and group size.

We may also collect optional details relevant to your trip, such as dietary requirements, health conditions relevant to adventure activities, and accommodation preferences, which you choose to share with us.

02

Use of Information

The personal information we collect is used strictly to process and manage your tour reservation, communicate booking confirmations and itinerary details, coordinate with guides and drivers, and provide customer support before, during, and after your trip.

We do not use your personal information for unrelated marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and you may opt out of any promotional communication at any time.

03

Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. Your information is only shared with the local transport providers, guides, and accommodation partners directly involved in delivering the tour or activity you booked, and only to the extent necessary.

We may disclose personal information if required to do so by law, or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal process or protect the safety of our guests or staff.

04

Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses basic cookies to support core functionality, such as remembering your language preference and enabling the website to load correctly across pages.

We also use standard analytics tools to understand general website traffic patterns, such as which pages are viewed most and how visitors find our site. This data is aggregated and anonymized, and is not used to personally identify visitors.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some website features may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

05

Data Security & Rights

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Access to guest data is limited to team members who need it to fulfil your booking.

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request that we delete your data, subject to any legal or accounting obligations we must retain it for. To make such a request, contact us using the details below.
