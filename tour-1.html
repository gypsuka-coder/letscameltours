Grand Morocco Tour: North to South

8 Days / 7 Nights

Overview

Morocco's most complete north-to-south journey, connecting the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the ancient medina of Fes, a night in the Sahara, and the souks of Marrakech into one seamless private trip.

Tangier → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga Desert → Kasbah Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

Day by Day

Day 1
Arrival in Tangier
Airport pickup and transfer to your riad. Free evening to explore the port city.
Day 2
Tangier to Chefchaouen
Drive through the Rif Mountains to the Blue Pearl. Afternoon exploring the old medina.
Day 3
Chefchaouen to Fes
Scenic drive south into the imperial city of Fes.
Day 4
Fes Guided Tour
Full day exploring the Medina, tanneries, and Al Quaraouiyine, the world's oldest university.
Day 5
Fes to Merzouga
Long scenic drive via Midelt and the Ziz Valley palm groves, arriving at the desert edge.
Day 6
Camel Trek & Desert Camp
Sunset camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes, dinner and overnight in a traditional camp.
Day 7
Merzouga to Marrakech
Sunrise over the dunes, then drive via the Aït Ben Haddou kasbah and the High Atlas.
Day 8
Marrakech & Departure
Morning free for the souks and Jemaa el-Fnaa before your departure transfer.

Trip Highlights

  • Blue Pearl streets of Chefchaouen
  • Fes Medina, the world's largest car-free urban zone
  • Camel trekking & luxury desert camp in Erg Chebbi
  • UNESCO-listed kasbahs along the way
  • Vibrant Marrakech souks
