8-Day Grand Morocco Tour

8 Days / 7 Nights

Overview

A complete private journey through Morocco's north, from the blue streets of Chefchaouen and the ancient medina of Fes, into the Sahara for a night under the stars, then back through the Dades Valley to finish in Marrakech.

Day by Day

Day 1
Arrival & Drive to Chefchaouen
Pickup from Casablanca, a brief stop in Rabat for the Hassan Tower and Oudayas Kasbah, then on through the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen.
Day 2
Explore Chefchaouen
A full day to wander the blue-washed lanes of the medina, visit the kasbah, and take in this laid-back mountain town.
Day 3
Chefchaouen to Fes via Volubilis & Meknes
Drive to the Roman ruins of Volubilis, a short visit to the imperial city of Meknes, then arrive in Fes.
Day 4
Guided Tour of Fes
A full day exploring the Fes medina with a local guide: the tanneries, Al Quaraouiyine, and the traditional souks.
Day 5
Fes to Merzouga Desert
Drive south via Midelt and the Ziz Valley to the edge of the Sahara. Sunset camel trek into the dunes of Erg Chebbi, dinner and overnight at a desert camp.
Day 6
Merzouga to Dades Valley
Sunrise over the dunes, then drive via Rissani and the dramatic Todra Gorges to the Dades Valley.
Day 7
Dades to Marrakech via the High Atlas
Drive through the Valley of Roses, stop at the Aït Ben Haddou kasbah and Ouarzazate, then cross the High Atlas via Tizi n'Tichka pass to Marrakech.
Day 8
Marrakech & Departure
Morning free to explore the souks and Jemaa el-Fnaa, before your departure transfer.

Trip Highlights

  • Blue Pearl streets of Chefchaouen
  • Roman ruins of Volubilis
  • Fes Medina, the world's largest car-free urban zone
  • Camel trek & overnight desert camp in Erg Chebbi
  • Dramatic Todra Gorges & Dades Valley
  • UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou kasbah
  • Vibrant Marrakech souks & Jemaa el-Fnaa
