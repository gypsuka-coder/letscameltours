8-Day Morocco Tour from Casablanca - Sahara & Imperial Cities

A full loop of roughly 1,800 km, from the Rif Mountains in the north to the Sahara dunes and the High Atlas in the south.

Day by Day Itinerary

Click a day to expand the full route, details, and a photo from that stage of the trip.

01
Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen
~340 km ~5h drive
Arrival at Casablanca airport and a private transfer to Rabat, Morocco's capital, for a short orientation stop at the Hassan Tower and Kasbah des Oudayas. Continue north through the Rif Mountains, arriving in the Blue Pearl of Chefchaouen by evening.
02
Chefchaouen Free Exploration
On foot Full day
A full free day in Chefchaouen to explore entirely at your own pace — wander the Blue Pearl's blue-washed lanes on foot, browse local crafts in the medina, and photograph the town's famous alleyways however and whenever you like.
03
Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes
~200 km ~4h drive
Depart Chefchaouen and stop at the Roman ruins of Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage site, followed by a visit to the royal city of Meknes. Continue to Fes for the evening.
04
Fes Cultural Guided Tour (Official Local Guide)
On foot Full day
Guided tour with an official local guide through Fes el-Bali, the world's largest car-free urban zone, including the tanneries, the Al Quaraouiyine — the world's oldest university — and the ancient medersas.
05
Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Ziz Valley → Merzouga Desert (Camel Trek & Luxury Camp)
~460 km ~8h drive
Leave Fes for the mountain town of Ifrane, then wind through the Cedar Forest, home to Barbary macaques, before descending along the Ziz Valley's palm groves to the desert edge at Merzouga. In the late afternoon, mount your camel for a sunset trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes, followed by dinner and an overnight stay in a luxury desert camp.
06
Merzouga Desert → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley
~320 km ~6h drive
Sunrise over the dunes before breakfast and a camel ride back to camp. Drive via the old caravan town of Rissani, then into the dramatic Todra Gorge with its towering rock walls, ending the day in the rose-hued Dades Valley.
07
Dades Valley → Rose Valley → Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech
~360 km ~7h drive
Drive through the fragrant Rose Valley and the film studios of Ouarzazate, then visit the UNESCO-listed kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou. Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, descending into Marrakech by evening.
08
Marrakech Guided Tour (Official Local Guide) & Departure Transfer
On foot Morning + transfer
Guided morning tour with an official local guide through Marrakech's highlights — the Koutoubia Mosque, Bahia Palace, and the souks around Jemaa el-Fnaa — before your private departure transfer to Marrakech Menara Airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour really private?

Yes — you'll have your own private vehicle and driver throughout, with no other travelers joining your itinerary.

Which days include an official local guide?

An official licensed guide is included strictly on Day 4 (Fes) and Day 8 (Marrakech). On other days your private driver leads orientation walks and stops, but licensed guiding is limited to these two cities on every itinerary.

How much does the tour cost?

Pricing is fully custom based on group size, travel dates, and accommodation tier — contact us for custom rates and current availability.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Absolutely — extend stays, add stops, or adjust the route to fit your schedule and interests.
