Overview

This Private Surf Lesson pairs you with a dedicated instructor who works with you (or a maximum of two people) for the entire session — full focus, personalized feedback on every wave, and coaching adapted specifically to your goals, from complete beginners wanting confident first waves to improvers refining technique.

Your instructor picks you up from your hotel and drives you to whichever surf spot around Taghazout and Agadir best matches the day's wave conditions and your level — no compromises made for a larger group. Because it's one-on-one, feedback is immediate and every wave becomes a coaching opportunity, meaning faster progress and quicker results than a group class.

After the session, unwind with a traditional Moroccan mint tea break before your instructor drives you back to your hotel.
