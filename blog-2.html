Best Time to Visit Morocco: Merzouga Desert vs. Taghazout Coast

August 2026

Back to Blog

Morocco's desert and its Atlantic coast run on very different calendars. If your trip includes both Merzouga and Taghazout, here's how to time it right.

Merzouga Desert Seasons

October to April is the sweet spot for the Sahara, warm days, cool nights, and none of the extreme summer heat that can make midday activities in Merzouga uncomfortable. December and January bring the coldest desert nights, so pack layers even if the days feel warm.

Taghazout Surf Seasons

Taghazout surfs well year-round, which is part of what makes it famous. Autumn and winter (September to February) tend to bring the biggest, most consistent swells and suit more experienced surfers, while spring and summer offer smaller, friendlier waves that are ideal for beginners.

Combining Both in One Trip

Because the desert prefers cooler months and Taghazout works well nearly year-round, October through April is generally the easiest window to combine both regions comfortably, cool enough for the Sahara, and still good surf on the coast.

Quick Month-by-Month Guide

  • Sept–Nov: Great all-round, mild desert heat, strong early-season swell in Taghazout
  • Dec–Feb: Coldest desert nights, but the best surf season on the coast
  • Mar–May: Comfortable everywhere, wildflowers in the Atlas foothills
  • Jun–Aug: Hot in the desert during the day, mellow beginner-friendly waves in Taghazout
