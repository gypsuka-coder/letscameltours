Overview

Essaouira is Morocco's laid-back Atlantic port town — a walled, UNESCO-listed medina of blue-and-white lanes, wood workshops, seagulls, and constant sea wind. It's an easy, scenic full-day escape from Agadir or Taghazout.

Your driver-guide picks you up from your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout in a private, air-conditioned vehicle, following the scenic coastal road with a stop to spot the tree-climbing goats and visit an argan oil cooperative. Once in Essaouira, you're free to explore the ramparts, medina, port, and beach at your own pace before heading back in the evening.

Pickup is at 08:30 AM from Agadir hotels and 08:50 AM from Taghazout, with drop-off back at your hotel between 5:30 and 6:00 PM.
