Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Morocco trip.

Marrakech is the most common starting point for our multi-day tours, but many itineraries can also start or end in Fes, Casablanca, or Tangier. Our day excursions and activities run from Marrakech, Agadir, and Taghazout.

Yes, absolutely. Many travelers pair a multi-day desert tour with a surf lesson or camel ride in Taghazout, or add a day trip to Essaouira or Marrakech before or after. Tell us your dates and we'll build a combined itinerary.

Surf lessons include a board, wetsuit, and an instructor for beginners and intermediates alike. Camel rides include a local guide and all safety equipment. Both can be booked as standalone half-day activities in Taghazout.

Yes. We offer private transfers to and from Agadir airport, as well as between cities, riads, and hotels. Transfers can be booked on their own or added to any tour.

All of our multi-day tours are fully private, with your own vehicle and driver-guide. Some excursions and activities, like surf lessons, may involve a small group, which we'll always tell you upfront.

For multi-day tours, we recommend booking at least a few weeks ahead, especially in high season. Day excursions and activities can often be arranged with just 24 to 48 hours' notice, depending on availability.
