Morocco Desert Tours FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking your Sahara adventure.

Most travelers spend 2 to 4 days on a desert tour, enough time to reach Merzouga or Zagora, ride camels into the dunes, and sleep in a desert camp. Longer circuits of 7 to 10 days combine the desert with imperial cities.

Merzouga has the tallest dunes and the most classic Sahara scenery, ideal if you have 3 or more days. Zagora is closer to Marrakech and better suited to shorter 2-day trips.

October to April offers the most comfortable temperatures, with cool nights and warm days. Summer months can be very hot during the day but nights remain pleasant in the desert.

Merzouga is roughly 9 to 10 hours by road from Marrakech, while Zagora is closer at around 6 hours. Most tours break up the drive with stops at kasbahs, gorges, and mountain passes.

Transport, an English-speaking driver-guide, accommodation, a camel trek at sunset, and dinner and breakfast in the desert camp are typically included. Lunches and personal expenses are usually extra.
