Overview

Paradise Valley is a hidden gem in the foothills of the Atlas Mountains, just outside Agadir — a lush oasis of crystal-clear natural pools, palm groves, and dramatic rock formations carved by the Tamraght River. It's one of the most refreshing day trips near Agadir and Taghazout.

Your driver-guide picks you up from your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout in a private, air-conditioned vehicle for the scenic drive into the valley. Once there, your English-speaking guide leads you on a leisurely walk through the palm groves to the natural swimming pools, with time to swim, relax, and take photos, plus a stop at a local Berber village before a traditional Moroccan lunch.

Pickup is at 8:30 AM from Agadir and Taghazout hotels, with drop-off back at your hotel around 3:00 PM. This is a shared group tour with up to 20 guests.
