A deposit of 30% of the total tour price is required to confirm your booking. Your reserved dates, guide, and vehicle are held once this deposit is received, not before. The remaining balance is due before your tour begins, or on the first day of your tour, as agreed in your booking confirmation.

We accept payment in cash or by card, in Euros (EUR) or Moroccan Dirhams (MAD). Card payments may carry a small processing fee, which we'll always tell you about before you pay. Prices are quoted per group for the itinerary agreed at booking and are only guaranteed once your deposit has been received.

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations made 30 days or more before your tour start date receive a full refund of any payments made, minus the deposit.
  • Cancellations made between 15 and 29 days before your tour start date receive a 50% refund of the total tour price.
  • Cancellations made within 14 days of your tour start date, and no-shows, are not eligible for a refund.

Rather than lose it outright, a deposit that would otherwise be forfeited under this policy can instead be held as credit toward a new booking made within 12 months of your original tour date, just let us know that's what you'd prefer. Refund requests must be submitted in writing, by email or WhatsApp, and approved refunds are processed within 7-14 business days to the original payment method.

We know plans shift, so we try to stay flexible. Date, route, or itinerary changes requested 7 or more days before your tour start date are made free of charge, subject to your new dates being available for a guide and vehicle.

Requests made within 15 days of departure are handled on a best-effort basis. By that point, guides, drivers, and accommodation are often already locked in for your dates, so we can't always guarantee a change is possible, but we'll do what we can.
