01

Booking & Confirmation

All bookings can be made directly through our website, by email, or via WhatsApp. A booking is only considered confirmed once a deposit has been received and a written confirmation (email or WhatsApp message) has been sent by Let's Camel Tours.

A non-refundable deposit of 20-30% of the total tour price is required to secure your reservation, unless otherwise agreed in writing. The remaining balance is due before or at the start of the tour, as specified in your booking confirmation.

It is the traveler's responsibility to ensure that names, dates, and group sizes provided at the time of booking are accurate, as these details are used to arrange guides, drivers, and accommodation.

02

Payment Terms

Payments can be made in cash or by card in Euros (EUR) or Moroccan Dirhams (MAD). Card payments may be subject to a small processing fee, which will be communicated before payment is taken.

Unless otherwise stated, the remaining balance of your tour is payable in cash to your guide or driver on the first day of the tour. Prices quoted are per person or per group as specified in your itinerary and are valid at the time of booking only.

Optional extras (camel rides, quad biking, spa treatments, additional meals, etc.) are not included in the base tour price unless specified, and are paid separately, directly at the point of service.

03

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the tour start time are eligible for a full refund, minus any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred (e.g. accommodation deposits).
  • Cancellations made between 24-48 hours before the tour start time are eligible for a 50% refund of the total tour price.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the tour start time, or no-shows, are non-refundable.
  • The original deposit paid to secure a booking is non-refundable in all cancellation scenarios, except where Let's Camel Tours cancels the tour.

Refund requests must be submitted in writing by email or WhatsApp. Approved refunds are processed within 7-14 business days, using the same payment method originally used.
