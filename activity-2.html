Overview

Experience the thrill of quad biking along Banana Beach and the Tamraght river trails, just outside Agadir and Taghazout. Suitable for both beginners and experienced riders, this exciting adventure takes you off the beaten path and into Morocco's raw natural landscapes.

Your driver picks you up from your hotel and transfers you to the starting point, where a professional quad instructor gives you a full orientation and safety briefing and fits your helmet and goggles before setting off. The 1-hour guided ride winds along Banana Beach and the Tamraght river trails, with plenty of stops for photos and panoramic ocean views, and the instructor takes photos and videos of your ride along the way.

After the ride, unwind with traditional mint tea and snacks before your driver takes you back to your hotel.
