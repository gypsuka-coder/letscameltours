Overview

Experience the thrill of quad biking across Agadir and Taghazout's varied terrain — from soft coastal sand dunes to rustic Berber countryside and fragrant argan forests. Suitable for both beginners and experienced riders, this adrenaline-filled adventure takes you off the beaten path and into Morocco's raw natural landscapes.

Your driver picks you up from your hotel and transfers you to the starting point, where a professional quad instructor gives you a full orientation and safety briefing and fits your helmet and goggles before setting off. The 2 to 3-hour guided ride winds through Berber villages, argan forests, and coastal sand dunes, with plenty of stops for photos and panoramic ocean views.

After the ride, unwind with traditional mint tea and snacks before your driver takes you back to your hotel.
