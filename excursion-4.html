Timlaline Dunes Day Trip & Outdoor Dinner in a Banana Valley from Agadir & Taghazout

A full-day adventure from Agadir and Taghazout through a painted village, a canyon, and the sand dunes of Timlaline, into a banana valley for dinner

Overview

Aghroud is a small village not far from Taghazout, where local artists have painted the houses in bright, bold colors, a fun and photogenic stop on the way to Timlaline, home to both a scenic canyon and a stretch of sand dunes.

Your driver picks you up from your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout and drives along the coast, passing through Taghazout's surf village, before heading inland to Aghroud. After some time exploring and photographing the painted village, you'll continue to Timlaline: first a stop to visit the canyon, then on to the sand dunes for quad biking, sandboarding, and your choice of a camel ride or a horse ride.

As the sun starts to set, there's a stop for traditional Moroccan mint tea, before the day winds down with an outdoor Moroccan dinner in a banana valley in Tamri, a village known for growing bananas, with a live folklore show depending on group size and restaurant capacity. Pickup time: 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM. Drop-off is back at your hotel in the evening.

Itinerary

How your day unfolds

1

Hotel Pickup, Ready for Adventure

Pickup from your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout in a private, air-conditioned vehicle. Pickup time: 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.

2

A Scenic Drive Through Taghazout

A scenic drive along the coast, passing through Taghazout's fishing and surf village.

3

Aghroud's Painted Village

A stop to explore and photograph Aghroud, a small village where local artists have painted the houses in bright colors.

4

Canyon Visit at Timlaline

A stop to explore Timlaline's canyon, carved into the desert landscape, before continuing to the sand dunes.

5

Quad Biking on the Sand Dunes

Arrive at Timlaline's sand dunes for a quad biking session across the terrain.

6

Camel or Horse, the Choice Is Yours

Choose either a camel ride or a horse ride across the sand dunes, included in the excursion. Let us know your preference when booking.

7

Sandboarding Down the Dunes

Slide down Timlaline's sand dunes on a sandboard, included as part of the day's activities.

8

Sunset Mint Tea in the Desert

As the light turns golden, stop for a glass of traditional Moroccan mint tea while the sun sets over the dunes.

9

An Outdoor Dinner in Tamri's Banana Valley

End the day with an outdoor Moroccan dinner in a banana valley in Tamri, a village known for growing bananas, with a live folklore show depending on group size and restaurant capacity.

10

Drop-off at Your Hotel

Return drive and drop-off at your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout.
