Overview

The Color Village of Aghroud is a small hillside village not far from Taghazout, where local artists have painted the rocks and boulders in bright, bold colors, a fun and photogenic stop on the way to the Timlaline sand dunes. Timlaline itself is a stretch of golden dunes close to Agadir, popular for quad biking, camel rides, and horse riding without the long drive out to the Sahara.

Your driver picks you up from your hotel in Agadir or Taghazout and drives along the coast, passing through Taghazout's surf village, before heading inland to Aghroud. After some time exploring and photographing the Color Village, you'll continue to Timlaline, where quad biking and your choice of a camel ride or a horse ride are included. Sandboarding and a buggy session are available as optional paid add-ons if you'd like more time on the dunes.

The day winds down with an outdoor Moroccan dinner at a local restaurant, with a live folklore show included depending on group size and restaurant capacity. Pickup time: [TBD], with drop-off back at your hotel in the evening.
