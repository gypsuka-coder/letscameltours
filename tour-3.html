Cultural Highlight

Imperial Cities & Coastal Pearl

6 Days / 5 Nights

Overview

A culture-first journey through Morocco's imperial capitals, Casablanca, Rabat, Meknes, and Fes, before ending in the vibrant streets of Marrakech.

Casablanca → Rabat → Meknes → Fes → Marrakech

Day by Day

Day 1
Arrival in Casablanca
Visit the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest in the world, then transfer to Rabat.
Day 2
Rabat City Tour
Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, and the Kasbah of the Oudayas.
Day 3
Rabat to Meknes
En route, stop at the Roman ruins of Volubilis before reaching Meknes.
Day 4
Meknes to Fes
Short drive to Fes, free afternoon to settle in.
Day 5
Fes Guided Tour
Full day in the Medina, tanneries, and traditional artisan workshops.
Day 6
Fes to Marrakech & Departure
Flight or drive to Marrakech for your onward departure.

Trip Highlights

  • Hassan II Mosque
  • Hassan Tower & Oudayas Kasbah
  • Volubilis Roman ruins
  • Fes traditional tanneries
  • Majorelle Garden
