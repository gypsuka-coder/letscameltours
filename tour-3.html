12-Day Morocco Tour: Casablanca to Marrakech & Sahara

12 Days / 11 Nights: Four Imperial Cities, Chefchaouen, the Sahara & the Atlantic Coast

A complete private tour from Casablanca through Rabat, the blue town of Chefchaouen, Fes, and the Middle Atlas to the dunes of Merzouga, then back across the High Atlas through Ouarzazate to Marrakech, with a day trip to Essaouira on the coast. Ends in Marrakech or Casablanca, your choice.

12-Day Casablanca - Sahara - Marrakech Route

A cross-country journey of roughly 2,900 km, from Casablanca north to Chefchaouen and Fes, south into the Sahara at Merzouga, then west over the High Atlas to Marrakech and the coast at Essaouira.

View Route on Google Maps
Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen → Fes → Merzouga Desert → Dades Valley → Ouarzazate → Marrakech → Essaouira · ~2,900 km

Day by Day Itinerary

Click a day to expand the full route, details, and a photo from that stage of the trip.

01
Arrival in Casablanca (Hassan II Mosque)
Airport pickup City tour
Your driver-guide meets you at Casablanca Airport for the transfer to your hotel. If time allows, explore Morocco's largest city and economic capital: the lively seafront Corniche and the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, set dramatically on the Atlantic. Lunch and a short city tour, then settle in for your first night in Morocco.
02
Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen
~341 km ~5-6h drive
Drive to Rabat, Morocco's political capital and one of its imperial cities, to see the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, and the Kasbah of the Udayas. After lunch, continue north via Ouazzane to Chefchaouen, the 'Blue Pearl' of the Rif Mountains, known for its striking blue-and-white streets. Overnight in a cozy riad in the medina.
03
Chefchaouen – Full Day Exploration
Free / guided day Full day
A full day to enjoy the ambiance of Chefchaouen. Stroll through the historic medina, with its famous blue doors and whitewashed walls, then hike up to the Spanish Mosque in the afternoon for a panoramic view over the town cradled in the Rif Mountains. Second night in your riad.
04
Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes
~195 km ~4-5h drive
One last stroll through Chefchaouen's narrow streets before driving to Volubilis, Morocco's best-preserved Roman ruins, a UNESCO World Heritage site with striking mosaics and ancient pillars. Continue to Meknes, another imperial city, to see the monumental Bab Al-Mansour gate and the Mausoleum of Moulay Ismail, before arriving in Fes for an overnight stay in a traditional riad.
05
Fes Medina Guided Tour
Local guide Full day
Discover the spiritual heart of Morocco with a knowledgeable local guide: the ancient Medina and its winding alleyways, the Al-Qarawiyyin University, one of the oldest in the world, and the Bou Inania Madrasa. See the famous Chouara tanneries, wander the souks and artisan quarters, then walk past the seven gates of the Royal Palace and through the Mellah, the old Jewish quarter, before a hilltop view over the whole medina at sunset.
06
Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga
~480 km ~6-8h drive
Head into the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping first in Ifrane, often called Morocco's 'Switzerland' for its alpine chalets, then the cedar forest near Azrou, home to friendly Barbary macaques. After lunch in Midelt, follow the palm-lined Ziz Valley and the Tafilalet oasis, with stunning panoramic viewpoints along the way, before arriving in Merzouga village in the late afternoon, welcomed with a glass of mint tea.
07
Merzouga – Camel Trek & Desert Camp (Khamlia & Rissani)
4x4 & camel Full day
Visit Khamlia village and its Gnawa musicians of Malian heritage to learn about their way of life and music, then head out to meet a family living in nomadic tents in the desert, sharing a traditional Berber pizza called madfouna. Later, browse the market of Rissani, a fortified trading town, before returning to Merzouga for a camel trek of around 1.5 hours into the heart of the dunes to watch the sunset. Dinner in the desert camp and an overnight stay beneath the stars.
08
Merzouga → Todra Gorges → Dades Valley
~266 km ~4-5h drive
Wake early to watch the sunrise over the dunes of Erg Chebbi, then breakfast at the camp before leaving Merzouga via Rissani and Erfoud, home to fascinating fossil workshops, on to Tinghir. Visit the towering, narrow Todra Gorges, among the most spectacular canyons in Morocco, with time for a walk beneath its sheer rock walls. In the afternoon, cross the Dades Valley, known for the 'Monkey Fingers' rock formations, stopping at the Castle Sands for photos, before dinner and an overnight stay in a riad overlooking the Dades Gorges.
09
Dades Valley → Ouarzazate → Ait Ben Haddou → High Atlas → Marrakech
~330 km ~5-6h drive
Drive via Ouarzazate to the UNESCO World Heritage Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, a spectacular fortified village that has served as the backdrop for Hollywood films such as 'Gladiator' and 'Lawrence of Arabia'. Cross the High Atlas Mountains at Tizi n'Tichka, the highest pass in the range at 2,260 m, with stops at panoramic viewpoints and an optional visit to an argan oil cooperative, before arriving in Marrakech in the afternoon.
10
Marrakech Guided City Tour
Local guide Full day
Explore Marrakech, another of Morocco's imperial cities, starting with the Ben Youssef Quranic School and the Koutoubia Mosque. Visit the Saadian Tombs, resting place of Sultan Ahmed al-Mansour, and the ornately decorated Bahia Palace. Stop at the Majorelle Garden, a serene oasis of exotic plants, before an afternoon in the Old Medina among the artisan workshops, finishing at the bustling Jemaa el-Fna square with its storytellers, musicians, and food stalls.
11
Marrakech → Essaouira Day Trip
~182 km ~3-4h drive
A day trip to the coastal city of Essaouira, perched on a small rocky peninsula on the Atlantic. Its orderly medina streets give the town a distinct character, and the fishing port is a lively spectacle as boats return with the day's catch. Enjoy the nearly 10 km stretch of fine sandy beach before heading back to Marrakech in the afternoon for an overnight stay in your riad or hotel.
12
Departure – Return to Casablanca or Marrakech
Up to ~379 km Depends on flight
Your final day depends on your flight schedule. If ending in Casablanca, your driver transfers you to Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport, roughly 4.5 hours from Essaouira/Marrakech. If ending in Marrakech, you'll be transferred to Marrakech Menara Airport or dropped off at your hotel or riad, as requested.
