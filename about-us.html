Our Story

Born and Raised in the Dunes of Merzouga

I'm Yassmina — a wife, a mother, and, at heart, a daughter of Merzouga. This small corner of Morocco isn't just where I'm from — it's where my whole family's story lives.

I grew up watching my father, Idir, known locally as "Idir of the Desert" (Idir2Desert), take travelers into the dunes he's known since he was a boy. For nearly 40 years, he has guided and driven visitors through this landscape — a career that began in the 1970s and has touched thousands of travelers since.

That upbringing shaped me. I went on to study Hotel and Tourism Marketing at Morocco's Institut Supérieur de Tourisme, under the Ministry of Tourism, because I wanted to turn what I'd grown up with into something I could build on.

Let's Camel Tours exists to carry that knowledge forward — the routes, the contacts, the small details only a local family with decades in this desert would know. It's also something I hope to pass on one day, the same way it was passed to me.

My husband adds another piece to that network — he's from Taghazout, on the Atlantic coast, and through his family and contacts there we're now able to extend that same local, trusted approach to the Taghazout region too.

Above all, I started this agency for travelers who want to see the real Morocco — not a version smoothed over for tourists, but the one we actually know, because we've lived here all along.
