Our Story

Since 2019, We've Been Sharing Our Morocco

Let's Camel Tours started with a simple idea: show travelers the Morocco we grew up in, not a rehearsed version of it. From the blue lanes of Chefchaouen to the golden dunes of Merzouga, every itinerary is built by people who actually know these roads.

We coordinate every trip from booking to the moment you land — working closely with trusted local drivers and guides so you're taken care of at every step.
