Our Story

Nestled in the Heart of Merzouga

I'm Yassmina. a wife, a mother, and at heart, a proud Amazigh daughter of Merzouga. This golden corner of Morocco isn't just where I'm from; it's where my whole family's story has lived for generations.

I grew up watching my father, Idir, affectionately known as "Idir of the Desert" (Idir2Desert), welcome international travelers into the dunes he has known since boyhood. Since the 1970s, with nearly 40 years of field experience as a trusted local guide and driver, he has shared the soul of our land with thousands of visitors.

That upbringing inspired my passion for hospitality. To turn our living legacy into a seamless experience for modern travelers, I earned my degree in Hotel and Tourism Marketing from Morocco's official tourism Institut.

Let's Camel Tours was created to carry this deep-rooted knowledge forward, the secret routes, the trusted contacts, and the thoughtful details that only a family with decades in the desert could master. With my husband's roots and network spanning the Atlantic coast in Taghazout, we seamlessly connect the quiet majesty of the Sahara with the vibrant spirit of the ocean.

We created this agency for travelers who seek the real Morocco, guided by a local family who has lived it all along, and dedicated to passing this heritage down to the next generation.

We named it Let's Camel Tours on purpose. The camel has carried our family across these dunes for generations, patient and steady, and it felt right to put that animal, and everything it represents, at the heart of our name.
